BETA. This is a work in progress and it does not finish a playthrough yet. Expect it to
get stuck, wander, and make poor decisions.

An agent that plays Terraria with classical AI rather than a neural network. It searches
for routes over what the body can stand on, works out what to gather and craft from a
progression graph, and drives the character by pressing the same keys a player would. It
never teleports and never reads anything the player's own map has not revealed.

Two switches on the panel. Driving hands your character to the agent, and turning it off
gives it back at once. Invulnerable stops the character taking damage, which is on by
default for now.

Client side only. It changes nothing for other players and adds no items, tiles or enemies.

Source, issues and progress: https://github.com/michaelcox3/terragent
